# V2 Ideas

- Multi-tenant organizations and roles: needed for real accounting teams with multiple reviewers.
- Custom field templates: useful once users want more document types or company-specific fields.
- DOCX export: helpful for audit packets and client-ready reports.
- Audit log of edits: required for compliance-heavy finance workflows.
- Analytics dashboard: show auto-approval rate, time saved, and common validation failures.
- Contracts and forms document types: expand beyond invoices after V1 is stable.
- Webhook and ERP integrations: push approved invoices into downstream tools.
- Domain-specific customization: tune prompts and validation by industry.
- Bounding-box highlight improvements: make review faster once extractor returns reliable coordinates.
- RabbitMQ migration: consider if Redis queue semantics become limiting.
- WebSocket or SSE live updates: replace polling when the app has more users.
